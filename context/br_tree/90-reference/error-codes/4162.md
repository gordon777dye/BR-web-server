**Error:** 4162

**Description:** A process belonging to another user tried to reach the assistant channel

**Details:** The channel is loopback-only and belongs to the user running the session. This is the refusal when the connecting process belongs to another user, or when its owner could not be read at all - an unreadable owner is treated as foreign rather than trusted.

**Raised by:** `harness.cpp`
