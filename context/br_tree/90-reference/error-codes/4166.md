**Error:** 4166

**Description:** The assistant's own commands may not raise the keyboard grant - only the person at the session may

**Details:** A line that came **from the assistant** ran `MSG("grant", n)` with `n` other than 0. The assistant may give the keyboard back - `MSG("grant", 0)` is allowed and is how it releases one - but it may not raise or extend its own grant. A grant is given at the session, by the person there.

**Raised by:** `numfunct.cpp`
