**Error:** 4619

**Description:** Reconnection timeout - will retry

**Details:** A **retryable** timeout while the client is reconnecting to its server, as distinct from `BREABORTRECONNECT`, which is the hard failure. `remote.cpp` raises it in one place and tests for it in nine more, each a point where the reconnect may be attempted again. Seeing it does not mean the session is lost.

**Raised by:** `remote.cpp`
