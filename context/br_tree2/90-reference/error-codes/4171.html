4171

Error: 4171

Description: A line the assistant sent used the AI command

Details: Two causes. A turn is the person's to start, and a reply is how one ends. A line the assistant sent that runs AI, including AI NEW, is refused with this error. Also raised when a turn is already running and two requests are already waiting behind it: a request made during a turn waits (up to two), and the fourth is refused. See 4169.

Name: BREAITURNNOTYOURS

Source: BR 5.0 wberrdefs.h, BREAITURNNOTYOURS: "this AI request was not started: a turn is the person's to start, and only two may wait behind the one running".
