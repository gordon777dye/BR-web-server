The 2010 Business Rules Group Conference will be held April 22, 2010 to April 24, 2010 in Boston, Massachusetts at the Embassy Suites hotel near the Boston-Logan airport. You will NOT want to miss this one! It will be truly special for many reasons.

Among many other things, Boston is the home of -

  • American History
  • Boston University
  • the Celtics
  • Paul Revere’s house
  • the Old North Church
  • “Old Iron Sides” - the USS Constitution
  • the Big Dig
  • Boston Common
  • Union Oyster House (est 1826)
  • Faneuil Hall and Market Place
  • JFK Presidential Library and Museum
  • Bunker Hill Monument
  • Freedom Trail
  • Boston Public Gardens
  • The Old Custom House Tower
  • Alcott House
  • Knotting Mill Museums
  • Hawthorne’s Wayside

Start planning now. This would be an excellent conference to meet up with your family afterward for some wonderful sightseeing in beautiful Boston. There are many sights (and sites) to see, many wonderful places to eat and shop, and rich American history to soak into your pores! Did you see the John Adams mini—series on TV? Come see where many of those events took place! No need to rent a car – there is an excellent subway system.

We will be dismissed roughly at noon on Saturday April 24th, so you can catch a same-day flight home without another hotel night's stay OR you can head into town (with any family members present) to experience lunch in the heart of Boston.

More details will be released closer to registration time after the first of the year. Put these dates on hold as this is the only scheduled Business Rules Group conference for 2010. We hope it will be the biggest yet and that means YOU must be there!
